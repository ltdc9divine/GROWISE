Startup: Not provided
Offer: Not provided
Business industry: Consumer product
Target customers: University students
Monthly budget: 2,000,000 ₫
Marketing objective: Build brand awareness
Current marketing channels: Just starting — no channels yet

Suggested allocation:
Content & creative: 900,000 ₫ (45%)
Paid promotion: 500,000 ₫ (25%)
Customer research: 300,000 ₫ (15%)
Testing & reserve: 300,000 ₫ (15%)

First test: 7–14 days
Primary metric: Reach & saves

Priorities:
1. Choose one main channel where your target customers already spend time; do not launch everywhere at once.
2. Consider relevant student communities, short useful content, and low-cost activities; choose places where your intended students actually participate.
3. Create useful content that explains the problem you solve and why your offer is relevant.
4. For an awareness test, choose a discovery signal such as relevant reach or profile visits. These show exposure, not whether people will buy.
5. Review after 7–14 days, write down what you learned, and avoid scaling a campaign based on one good day.
