Our story

Good ideas need more than good timing.

Early-stage founders may need to decide whom to serve, which channel to try, how to plan a limited budget, and what to measure. These are the practical questions this project is designed to help users explore; they are not presented as proven research findings.

The research topic is “Exploring Factors Affecting Start-up Success among HUFLIT Seniors: Developing a Marketing Support Website for Student Start-ups.” Growise is the website prototype developed for that project.

This prototype offers educational guidance and planning tools. It is not a professional marketing agency, does not verify market assumptions, and cannot guarantee business or campaign results.

Our mission

Make useful marketing knowledge and practical planning more accessible to student founders.

Our vision

Become a trusted starting point for students building thoughtful, sustainable ventures.

Our principles

Clarity over hype. Evidence over assumptions. Learning before scaling.

01 /

Marketing Strategy Consultation

If you are unsure whom to reach or which channel might fit, a future consultation could review the audience, goals, and constraints you provide. Intended output: a discussion outline with questions to investigate. Scope: this prototype does not provide professional market research; assumptions still need validation.Potential benefit: Clarify a possible audience, compare channel fit, and identify questions to validate before committing resources.

↗
02 /

Marketing Plan Development

If marketing tasks feel scattered, the proposed planning support could organize a stated goal into draft priorities, timing, content prompts, and review points. Intended output: an outline to adapt. Scope: this prototype does not run campaigns; any plan would depend on the information provided.Potential benefit: Turn a broad goal into an adaptable sequence of priorities and checkpoints.

↗
03 /

Budget Optimization

The planner generates an illustrative allocation from fixed rules to help you consider spending categories and small tests. Intended output: a draft allocation to review. Scope: it is not tailored financial advice or a guarantee; check your actual costs and margins before spending.Potential benefit: Make spending trade-offs visible and reserve room for learning from small tests.

↗
04 /

Campaign Performance Review

If campaign results are hard to interpret, a future review could examine data you provide—such as reach, inquiries, or sales—and outline a possible next test. Intended output: observations and a question to investigate. Scope: no data integrations; conclusions depend on the data and do not prove causation or predict performance.Potential benefit: Identify patterns in available campaign metrics and choose a next question to investigate.
